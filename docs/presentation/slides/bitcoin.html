Why Bitcoin

A public record no one can rewrite

What is Bitcoin?

Besides being a currency, Bitcoin is a worldwide public ledger kept by thousands of independent computers. Anything written into it gets a date, and can never be changed or removed, by anyone.

We use it as a public notary, not as money: no cryptocurrency is bought, held or paid, and no one needs a Bitcoin wallet.

Not even we can rewrite history

Records can't be backdated or quietly changed afterwards.

Proof that outlives us

Anyone can check it with free, open tools, even without Certyfi.

Private by design

Only a digital fingerprint is recorded, never names or details.

An analogy: it's like printing a sealed fingerprint of our records in a newspaper that thousands of people keep a copy of. Later, anyone can check that our records still match the fingerprint printed on that date. About once an hour Certyfi takes a fingerprint (a hash) of all new audit-trail entries and has it timestamped on Bitcoin through OpenTimestamps, a free, open standard; confirmation on the blockchain takes a few hours. The fingerprint reveals nothing about the certificates: it can't be turned back into names or data, it only proves the records existed unchanged at that time. The proof file can be downloaded from any certificate's verification page and checked with the standard OpenTimestamps tools, so it stays valid even if Ahamsys or Certyfi no longer existed. There is no cost per certificate and no cryptocurrency involved.
