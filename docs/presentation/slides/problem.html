The problem

Paper and PDF certificates are easy to fake and slow to check

Easy to forge

A scanned certificate or PDF can be edited with ordinary tools, and the copy looks genuine.

Slow to verify

Employers and universities email or phone the issuing institution and wait days for an answer.

One person can issue

Without a second check, a single staff member can issue a certificate that should never exist.

These are the three gaps Certyfi closes: forgery, slow verification, and unchecked issuance.
