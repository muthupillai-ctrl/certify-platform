Privacy and data protection

Personal data, handled with care

The institution stays in control

Each institution's data is kept separate and used only to issue and verify its certificates.

Only what a verifier needs

Public pages never show email addresses or internal notes.

Checkers stay anonymous

Who checks a certificate is never recorded, only that it was checked.

Erasure on request

Personal details can be erased without breaking the tamper-evident audit trail.

Nothing personal on Bitcoin

Only anonymous fingerprints are timestamped, never names or details.

Agreed with each institution

Data-protection terms, including where data is hosted, are agreed before it starts.

Designed around the principles of Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws across Africa.

The institution is the data controller for its students' data; Ahamsys processes it on the institution's behalf. Everything on this slide is how the platform works today, except the last card, which is how we work with each institution: we agree data-protection terms, including hosting location, before a pilot starts. Erasure: names, emails and reasons are kept beside the audit trail rather than inside its hash chain, behind salted fingerprints, so an institution can erase a person's details on request (for a revoked or rejected certificate) while the chain stays intact and verifiable; changing those details directly in the database is still detected by the integrity check. The verification page shows the certificate's own content (name, achievement, fields the institution chose) because that is what it is for; institutions tell students their certificates are publicly verifiable. We say "designed around the principles" rather than "compliant": formal compliance also depends on each institution's own registration and policies.
