Free during the pilot phase. Everything happens in the browser, with nothing to install. Step 4 is optional.

What a pilot looks like. Step 1 is done by us, the platform operator: we create the institution's own separate workspace and its first administrator. From step 2 the institution works on its own: the administrator adds the logo, colours and public profile, and invites registrars and approvers (at least two people, because every certificate needs a second person to approve it). Step 3 uses the visual template designer for certificates and for marks cards with subject-wise results. Step 4 brings years of paper certificates online from an Excel or CSV file, if the institution has them. Step 5: registrars start issuing and approvers approve; systems such as LMSpromise or Montessori360 can also issue through the Partner API.
