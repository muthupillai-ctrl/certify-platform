Anyone can check a certificate in seconds

Free for anyone, with no account and no phone call. Every check shows the official record and its status (active, suspended, revoked or expired), and the browser checks the signature itself.

4

Check a digital credential file (Open Badges 3.0)

5

Optional: download the Bitcoin proof and check it independently

Five ways in, one answer. The QR code carries a signed summary, so the Certyfi Verify app can confirm a certificate with no internet connection. Paper numbers need the name as printed because old numbers are often sequential and easy to guess. The verification page shows only what a verifier needs: personal contact details and internal notes are never shown, certificates that haven't been approved can't be looked up, and who checked is never recorded; the institution sees only that and how its certificates were checked.
