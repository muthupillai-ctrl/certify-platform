How it works

From template to verified certificate

1

Design

Admin designs certificate and marks-card templates

2

Prepare

Registrar enters the recipient and details

3

Approve

A different person reviews and approves

4

Sign

Signed with the institution's key and recorded

5

Verify

Anyone scans the QR code, no login needed

After issue, suspending and revoking happen on a separate, deliberate page, and revoking needs a second approver too. Expiry is shown automatically.

Walk left to right. Steps 2 and 3 must be different people. Every step from preparation onwards is written to the audit trail at the moment it happens. Suspension takes effect at once and can be undone; revocation is permanent, so one person asks and a different approver approves, and both must type the certificate's code to confirm.
