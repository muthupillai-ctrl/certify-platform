Who uses it

Each role sees only what it needs

RoleWhat they do
Platform operatorOnboards institutions and oversees operations
Institution AdminUsers, templates, branding, API keys, reports and the audit trail
RegistrarPrepares certificates and imports paper records
ApproverApproves certificates and imports, and handles revocations
StudentSees, and where needed pays for, their own certificates in the student portal
VerifierAnyone checking a certificate, with no account
Permissions are enforced on the server for every action, not just hidden in the interface. Students have their own separate sign-in to the student portal and can never reach staff pages.
