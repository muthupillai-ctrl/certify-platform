Honest dating: each certificate shows its original paper date and states that it was digitally re-issued on the day it was imported.

For institutions with years of paper records. Up to 10,000 certificates per file; columns are matched automatically and nothing is created until every row has been checked. The signature and Bitcoin timestamp only vouch for the day of digitising, never pretending the record existed digitally in the past, and the paper date and number are part of what is signed, so neither can be changed afterwards. Employers holding an old paper certificate can verify it with its number and the name as printed.
