For the recipient

A student portal of their own

Their own sign-in

Set up from a personal emailed link, with "forgot password" built in.

Every certificate in one place

Old and new, from staff, paper records or a connected system.

An email at every step

When a certificate is requested, issued, rejected, suspended or revoked.

Pay, then it's issued

Where the institution charges, students pay by a due date, with a reminder first.

Institutions can invite all their past recipients to the portal at once.

Every recipient with an email becomes a student in Certyfi, so certificates issued years apart, or through different routes, sit together in one portal. Students sign in with their email; if it changes, staff or the connected system update it, and the old address is told. Emails go out at each step and never quote internal reasons for a suspension or revocation; they ask the student to contact the institution. Payment is optional and set per connected system: the certificate waits until the student pays, then goes to an approver as usual. Today the payment step is a test payment; a real payment provider comes next. If the due date passes, the request is cancelled automatically and the student is told. Staff can email a set-up link to one student, or to everyone not yet signed up, skipping anyone invited in the last week.
