A signed digital credential

An Open Badges 3.0 file for digital wallets, checkable anywhere without Certyfi.

For certificates and for marks cards with subject-wise results, all also in the recipient's own student portal.

Recipients are the ones who show certificates to employers, universities and visa offices, so what they receive matters as much as how it's issued. All four come from the certificate's verification page. The PDF includes the QR code, so even a printed copy can be checked. The LinkedIn button pre-fills the certification with the institution, title, dates and a link back to the verification page; LinkedIn itself doesn't verify anything, the link does. The digital credential follows the international Open Badges 3.0 standard, so badge wallets and other systems can read and check it.
