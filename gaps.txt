Gaps in the student and LMS flow (worth fixing before real students)

1. Revoked or suspended certificates don't notify the student (confirmed). This is the same problem the LMS found with rejections, one step later. If an issued LMS certificate is revoked or suspended, the student isn't emailed, though their portal shows it.

2. Unpaid certificates stay open forever. 
If a student never pays, the certificate sits as 
"Awaiting payment" indefinitely. 
There's no reminder email and no automatic 
cancel after a set number of days.

3. There's no way to change a student's email or name. 
If the LMS sends the same studentId with a new email, 
Certyfi refuses it with 409, and staff can't 
edit a student either. 
Students change emails, so this will come up.

4. The LMS has to keep checking. It learns about "paid", 
"approved" and "rejected" by polling. Webhooks, where 
Certyfi calls the LMS when a status changes, would 
make it immediate and cut the traffic.

5. There's no way to invite all existing students at 
once. Students created from older certificates have to 
be emailed one at a time from the Students page. 
A "Email set-up links to everyone not signed up" button 
would fix that.

Gaps for staff

6. Reports and the dashboard don't know about the new 
steps (confirmed).
  The staff-activity report has no "Paid" or "Cancelled"
   columns.
   The dashboard doesn't count certificates 
   awaiting payment.

7. There's no email history for a student. Staff can't 
see what was sent or whether it failed. Also, Certyfi 
records an email as "sent" once Resend accepts it, 
not when it reaches the inbox, so bounces aren't tracked.
